# Objection Matrix

For each objection, a response in each king's voice. Pick based on where the buyer's resistance is coming from.

## "We already have a [website / advisor / plan]"

- **Voss (label + wait):** "It sounds like you feel what you have is working well enough…" — stop. Wait for the correction.
- **Belfort (loop to threshold):** "Forget the cost for a second. If it was free, and it got you 3x the result, would you at least look?"
- **Miller (reframe identity):** "Most of the people we work with already had one. They just didn't have one that fought for them."

## "We don't have budget"

- **Voss (calibrated):** "How am I supposed to help you if budget is truly zero?"
- **Belfort (invert the cost):** "How much is the current situation costing you per month in customers you're not getting?"
- **Miller (stakes of inaction):** "The cost of doing nothing is usually higher than the cost of the fix. Here's the math…"

## "Send me info"

- **Voss (label the real reason):** "It sounds like you want to evaluate this without pressure. Totally fair. Here's the shortest possible version…"
- **Belfort (collapse to demo):** "Info PDFs end up in spam. Let me show you the 90-second version live — then you have the info *and* you've seen it work."
- **Miller (identity + next step):** "I can send a doc, but it won't do it justice. The demo *is* the doc."

## "I need to think about it"

- **Voss (calibrated):** "What specifically would you need to think about?"
- **Belfort (loop):** "When people say that, usually it means I haven't shown enough value yet. What's the piece that isn't clicking?"
- **Sandler-adjacent (negative reverse):** "Maybe this isn't the right fit. Walk me through what's making you hesitate."

## Federal-specific: "I've done my own research, I think I'm good"

- **Voss (accusation audit first):** "You've probably looked at OPM, checked the calculators, and done more homework than most of your coworkers."
- **Belfort (insight that crosses threshold):** "Here's what most of the self-directed folks miss: [GRB pension estimates can be off hundreds a month / HSA money left on the table / FEHB overspend]. You just want verifiable numbers."
- **Miller (guide posture, not hero):** "Your research gets you 90% of the way. The last 10% is the gap between retiring when you want and retiring when the math says you can."
