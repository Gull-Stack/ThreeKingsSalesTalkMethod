# Application: Gull-Stack SuperTool (PeterQuill playbook)

Distilled from `Gull-Stack/gullstack-site/sales-agent-blueprint.html` (March 2026).

## The unfair advantage

**"We already built your website."** No other agency does this. Do not pitch a PowerPoint. Ship them a finished demo site before they pay a dollar. Eliminates the #1 objection: *what will I actually get?*

Demo sites are the weapon. Always lead with them.

## The email formula (approved)

```
Subject: [Specific problem]

[Name],

[Specific problem — 1 sentence].

[Impact — who's winning instead of them — 1 sentence].

[Buried strengths — 2–3 things they have that competitors don't].

[Core insight — not losing to a better [X], losing to a better *presentation* of [X]].

Let's chat briefly. [Show what competitors in [location] are doing to match digital presence to excellence].

Peter Quill
GullStack
(720) 739-4085
```

Every line runs the Three Kings: accusation-audit opener (Voss), specific insight that crosses threshold (Belfort), hero-has-buried-strengths framing (Miller). Directive close, no "I," no questions.

## 4-touch sequence

| Day | Frame | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pattern interrupt** — "We built you a new website (not kidding)" + demo link | Belfort — absolute certainty |
| 3 | **Follow-up** — "Did you see it?" + social proof | Voss — labeled curiosity |
| 5 | **Challenger** — "Your website is costing you patients" + specific 404s / gaps | Belfort — scarcity |
| 7 | **Breakup** — "Closing the loop — taking the demo down in 48h" | Voss — no-oriented ("Would it be wrong to take it down?") |

The breakup email has the highest response rate. Do not skip it.

## Research depth

30 researched emails/day beats 100 generic emails/day. Per lead:

1. Website audit — specific problems (404s, thin content, positioning)
2. Competitor analysis — who's winning locally and why
3. Buried strengths — what the hero already has
4. The real dilemma — "losing to weaker competitors because of presentation"

## Zero Tolerance for Lies

The rule that makes everything else work. One lie = deletion.

- Do not claim something is done unless you can prove it (screenshot, URL, verified data).
- Do not report results you haven't verified.
- Do not say "imported to CRM" unless you logged in and counted.
- If unsure, say "I don't know." Never guess and present as fact.

The reason this is here, in a Three Kings doc: Voss + Belfort + Miller all collapse the moment one lie is caught. Trust is the substrate. Everything is built on it.
