# Jordan Belfort — Straight Line System

The principle: a sale is a straight line from open to close. Every second of the conversation is either moving toward the close or away from it. There is no neutral ground. Your job is to keep the prospect on the line — pull them back the instant they drift, then keep advancing toward the close.

## The whole system in one breath

A buyer says yes when **three certainties are all at a 10/10** (product, you, company) AND they are past their **action threshold** while their **pain** is present and **trust** is high. You raise those certainties through **tonality** (how you say it) more than logic (what you say), and you handle every objection by **looping** — never answering it literally, always using it to raise the weakest certainty, then closing again.

## The two thresholds

- **Action threshold** — how sure someone must be before they'll buy. Low-threshold buyers act on partial certainty; high-threshold buyers need near-total certainty. You raise certainty until you clear *their* bar.
- **Pain threshold** — people buy to move away from pain faster than toward pleasure. Surface the pain (without cruelty), amplify the cost of inaction, then present your solution as the relief.

## The four core seconds

You establish or destroy your authority in the **first four seconds**. From "hello" you must come across as three things at once:
1. **Sharp as a tack** — quick, on it, no fumbling.
2. **Enthusiastic as hell** — infectious certainty, not hype.
3. **An expert in your field** — an authority worth listening to.

Lose any of those in the opening and you spend the rest of the call climbing out of a hole.

## Where Belfort wins alone (vs. the other two kings)

Warm audiences, inbound, anyone already leaning in. The buyer needs a confident guide to step them across the threshold — not a therapist. Pair with **Voss** when the prospect is defensive/skeptical and needs to feel heard first; pair with **Miller** to frame the buyer as hero and name the villain. Belfort supplies the *spine* — direction and certainty — the other two soften the entry and the story.
