# Jordan Belfort — Straight Line System

The principle: every second of a sales conversation is either moving toward the close or away from it. No neutral ground. Your job is to keep the line straight.

## The Three Tens (a.k.a. thresholds)

For the buyer to say yes, all three must be at a 10/10:

1. **The product / idea** — do they believe this thing will solve their problem?
2. **The company** — do they trust that you will still be there to deliver?
3. **You** — do they trust *you* personally?

Low number on any one = the deal dies. Diagnose which and raise it before closing.

## Tonality (more than words)

Belfort claims the words are ~10% of the impact. Tonality and body language carry the rest.

Five high-leverage tones:
- **"I care / I want to know"** — soft, curious, slight upward lilt.
- **Scarcity** — lowered volume, urgency, implication of limited time.
- **Absolute certainty** — flat, declarative, no lilt. "This will work."
- **Reasonable man** — matter-of-fact, friend-to-friend.
- **Mystery / intrigue** — hushed, pulled-in, "lean closer."

## Looping

Objections are rarely objections. They are *requests for more certainty on one of the Three Tens.* Don't answer the objection literally. Loop it back into raising the relevant threshold.

- "I need to think about it" → usually product certainty < 10. Loop: "I get that. What's the one piece you'd need to feel 100% this solves the problem?"
- "It's too expensive" → usually company / risk < 10. Loop: "Fair. Set price aside — if it was free, would the fit be right?"

## Threshold of action

Every buyer has an action threshold. Insights drive people over it. The move: say something they *couldn't have known, or felt but never said aloud.* That is the crossing point.

## State management

Belfort is relentless about the seller's own state. Before every call: power pose, biggest-win recall, abundance mindset. You cannot transfer certainty you don't feel.

## When Belfort wins alone

Warm audiences. Inbound calls. Anyone already leaning in. The buyer needs a confident guide to step them across the threshold — not a therapist.
