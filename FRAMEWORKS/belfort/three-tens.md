# The Three Tens — the three certainties

For a buyer to say yes, **all three of these must be at a 10/10**:

1. **The product / idea** — do they truly believe *this thing* will solve their problem and deliver the outcome?
2. **You (trust + connection)** — do they trust *you* personally, like you, believe you have their interest at heart?
3. **The company** — do they trust the company behind you to be there, deliver, and stand behind it?

A buyer arrives at every conversation with each certainty already sitting somewhere on a 1–10 scale. **A low number on any one of the three kills the deal** — even if the other two are perfect. Your entire job in the body of the call is to move all three to a 10.

## Logical certainty vs. emotional certainty

Each of the three has **two layers** that must both be satisfied:
- **Logical certainty** — "this makes sense; the numbers/features/proof add up." Built with evidence, logic, proof, comparison.
- **Emotional certainty** — "I can *feel* that this is right; I want it now." Built with tonality, vision of the outcome, pain relief, future-pacing.

Logic alone gets a "let me think about it." Emotion alone gets buyer's remorse. You need both stacked on all three tens. Belfort's order: build **logical certainty first**, then layer **emotional certainty** on top to drive past the action threshold.

## Diagnose before you close

When a prospect hesitates, don't argue — **diagnose which of the three is low.** The objection tells you where:

- "I need to think about it" → usually **product** certainty < 10 (or fear of making a mistake).
- "It's too expensive" → usually **company/risk** or **value** certainty < 10 — they don't yet believe the outcome is worth it or that you'll deliver.
- "I've been burned before" / "I don't know you" → **you** or **company** certainty < 10.
- "Let me talk to my spouse/partner" → often genuine, but frequently a low number they're not naming — surface it.

Ask, identify the low number, raise *that specific certainty*, then re-close. Don't waste energy raising a certainty that's already a 10.

## How to raise each one

- **Product** → tailored benefits tied to *their stated pain*, proof, specificity, "which means…" emotional outcomes.
- **You** → rapport, tonality (care + sincerity), demonstrated listening, integrity, expertise shown not claimed.
- **Company** → track record, guarantees, social proof, longevity, "we'll be here."

## Threshold of action — the crossing point

Even with high certainty, a buyer must cross their **action threshold.** The move that pushes them over: deliver an **insight** — say something they couldn't have known, or something they felt but never said aloud. That earned insight is the lever that tips a hesitant buyer into action. Combine it with present pain and full certainty, and the close is the only natural next step.
