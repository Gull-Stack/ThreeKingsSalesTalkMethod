# State Management — the inner game

Belfort is relentless on this: **you cannot transfer a certainty you do not feel.** The prospect mirrors your state. If you're tentative, they get tentative. If you're in a state of absolute certainty, that certainty becomes contagious. Managing your own state is therefore not a warm-up — it's a core sales skill.

## Get into state before every contact

- **Anchor a peak state.** Recall your biggest win in vivid detail until you *feel* it, then fire it on cue (the classic NLP anchor — touch, breath, or phrase). Walk into the call already in that state.
- **Physiology drives emotion.** Posture, breathing, movement, voice. Power posture, full breath, stand up, smile before you dial. Change the body, change the state.
- **Focus.** Where attention goes, energy flows. Before the call, focus on the outcome and the value you bring, not on the fear of rejection.
- **Abundance, not scarcity.** Approach from "I have plenty of prospects; I'm choosing to help this one" — never from "I need this sale." Neediness reads instantly and tanks all three certainties about *you*.

## The states to run

- **Certainty** — the master state. Total belief in the product, the company, and yourself.
- **Clarity** — knowing exactly where you are on the line and where you're going next.
- **Confidence** — earned, calm, not bravado.
- **Courage** — to ask, to close, to loop again after a no.

## Standards and beliefs (the deep layer)

- **Raise your standards.** Your results match the standards you hold for yourself, not your wishes. Decide what's non-negotiable about your performance.
- **Limiting beliefs cap results.** Beliefs about money, selling, deserving, and rejection silently throttle the inner game. Surface them, challenge them, replace them. ("Selling is helping," not "selling is taking.")
- **Manage the meaning of "no."** Reframe rejection as data and as one step closer, never as a verdict on you. The seller who doesn't flinch at no out-earns everyone.

## The link back to tonality

State and tonality are one system. You can't fake the certainty tone for long if you're not in the certainty state — the prospect hears the gap. Get the state right and the right tonality comes out naturally. This is why state management *precedes* technique, not the other way around.
